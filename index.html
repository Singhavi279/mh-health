About the event

Where Maharashtra's health community meets

Swasthya Sangam is the meeting point for everyone who shapes the health of Maharashtra. Hospitals, doctors, pharma companies, diagnostics labs, MedTech innovators, wellness brands, insurers and AYUSH practitioners come together under one roof.

Presented by Maharashtra Times and Times Internet Languages Live, the event brings together four experiences in one place: a Summit for ideas, an Expo for solutions, Masterclasses for learning and Awards for recognition.

Our aim is simple: better health, easier access and stronger prevention for every family in Maharashtra.

Why “Sangam”? A sangam is a place where different streams meet. Health is the same. It works best when every part of the system comes together.
