About Swasthya Sangam

Swasthya Sangam 2026
7 December 2026 · Mumbai

Why we are bringing Maharashtra's health community into one room

Good health is built by many hands. Swasthya Sangam is where they finally meet.

The doctors who treat, the hospitals that build, the companies that innovate, the officials who enable and the families who depend on all of them rarely share a stage. Maharashtra Times created Swasthya Sangam to change that: one day in Mumbai, with the Summit, Masterclasses, Expo and Awards under one roof.

Every session is shaped by five ideas: People, Innovation, Access, Prevention and Impact. The aim is simple. Fewer speeches about healthcare, more answers someone can use on Monday morning.

We also want to celebrate the work that rarely makes the front page. Through the Swasthya Sangam Awards, we recognise the hospitals, doctors and health brands getting it right. If you know one, we want to hear about it.
